Frequently asked
questions.

Do clients need to create a developer account?+

No. Clients use a dedicated, email-based project access flow rather than a regular developer account.

Can clients approve work, not just requirements?+

Yes. Approval of the task scope and acceptance of the delivered result are distinct parts of the workflow.

Are AI agents included automatically?+

Dev2Done supports external AI agents through a permission-controlled API. Connecting and running an executor or AI provider requires separate setup.

Can I export a project specification?+

Yes. Export selected project tasks in PDF or DOCX, including optional details and estimates, with support for four document languages.

Is Dev2Done open for public registration?+

Not yet. The product is being prepared for public onboarding. Existing users can still log in through the application.
